01 · Batch overview

What foreman status and foreman next --all say, as the app's home screen: how far the batch has got, the next ticket, and the human ticket ahead. Every batch in the repo is in the rail. Data: foreman status --json <batch>, re-read when a file in the batch changes.

Sample: .scratch/human-tickets, 4 of 10 landed, 05 next, 07 a human ticket with 07a its followup.

Pick: a · dome and order. It reads top to bottom the way foreman status does — dome, next, list — so the two never disagree in shape. The order stays one list, because the loop works one ticket at a time; b's columns suggest lanes the loop never runs. c's stake line is lovely at 10 tickets and unreadable at 30; a keeps the stake line in miniature under the dome.
