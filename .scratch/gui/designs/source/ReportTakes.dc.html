07 · Report

foreman report <batch>: what the batch has cost and what came of it, from foreman-runs.jsonl. Free: it reads a file. The figures and their names are the report's own — iterations, landed, unproductive, checkpoint turns, limit hits, billed tokens, model-minutes, turns — via the new foreman report --json, so the app never does its own sums. Sample: the real totals of the finished terminal-design batch; the expanded iterations are illustrative.

Pick: a · the bill and the ledger. It is the terminal's report (10a) in a window: the bill with the big total and the outcome counts, then one ledger row per run with a cost bar. Batches have 1–20 runs; for that, b's charts are decoration that repeat the ledger's own numbers, and the bill is the picture the person already knows.
