05 · How a run ends: exit 0, 10, 20, 30

Each take shows all four endings. What the app knows comes from the run's footer in foreman-runs.log (# complete, # paused: … + # next up: …, # stopped: needs a human + the new # reason: …, # stopped: waiting on a human — …), and from the exit code when the app started the run itself. Reopened later, it shows the same thing, because it is all in the log.

Resuming is rerunning the same command, so Resume… opens the same confirmation as Start run. The free next steps (report, dry run, doctor) say so.

Pick: b · a scene for each, changed at sign-off. a was the first pick; at sign-off b was taken instead. Every ending has a Show iterations button, because the picture replaces the run's evidence and the evidence stays one click away.

Redrawn at the second sign-off, as b1. No confetti. Each scene is the terminal's sunrise (lib/foreman-draw.cjs): a big sun in flat bands on a low horizon, and the foreman as one flat dark shape in front of it, with no face and no colour. The figure is the terminal's own: the 18 × 25 half-block sprite from foreman help, drawn in square cells (the pixel silhouette, picked over a smooth figure in a hard hat and a pictogram). The pose says how the run ended: exit 0 an arm raised under the risen sun, the landed stakes flagged; exit 10 a hand held up, the sun held halfway; exit 20 scratching his head beside a leaning problem! sign on the right; exit 30 a shrug beside a leaning need human inspection! sign on the left, under exactly the same sun as exit 10.
